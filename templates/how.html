A CLEAR PATH TO A RESPONSE

How CampusCare works

A straightforward workflow keeps students informed and helps simulated campus responders coordinate next steps.

01＋

Report

Share the concern, choose a category and severity, and tell us where it is happening.

02⌕

Classify

Your report appears in the responder queue with the details needed for review.

03✓

Acknowledge

A simulated responder can acknowledge the report and add an update to its timeline.

04↗

Assign & respond

Responsibility and response progress can be recorded for students to follow.

05◉

Resolve

Once addressed, responders add a resolution summary and close the report.

!

CampusCare is a demonstration prototype, not an emergency service. It does not notify real responders, contact emergency services, or guarantee assistance. For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or campus security directly.
